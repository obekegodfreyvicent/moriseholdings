About Aldric Marine

Aldric Marine designs and builds propulsion and power-management systems for commercial vessels, offshore support ships, and naval fleets. The company operates three manufacturing sites and a global service network spanning fourteen ports.

Since Morise became the controlling shareholder, Aldric has expanded into hybrid-electric propulsion, opening a dedicated research facility in Gothenburg in 2019.

Governance

Aldric Marine operates with its own board and executive management. Morise holds three of seven board seats and is represented on the audit and remuneration committees.

The company reports separately under Dutch statutory requirements, with results consolidated into Morise's annual and half-year filings.
