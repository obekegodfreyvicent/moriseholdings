Who we are

A holding company with a culture that combines discipline and patience.

Overview

Building companies across generations

Morise Holdings Limited is a diversified holding company managing a portfolio of controlling and minority positions across precision engineering, healthcare, media, industrial materials, luxury goods, and energy.

Founded in 1961 by the Morise family, the group has grown from a single manufacturing concern into an eleven-company portfolio with a combined net asset value of £19.4 billion. The family remains the controlling shareholder through Morise Family Holdings B.V.

Approach

How we invest

We do not run our companies day to day. Each portfolio company keeps its own management team, brand, and operating independence — Morise's role is governance, capital allocation, and long-term stewardship.

01

Patient capital

We hold positions through full economic cycles rather than exit windows.

02

Active governance

Board representation in every company, without displacing operating management.

03

Permanent structure

No fund life or redemption pressure — capital that can wait.
