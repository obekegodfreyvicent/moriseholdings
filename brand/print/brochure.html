Who we are

We move value from source to shelf.

Morise Holdings Limited is a Ugandan holding company with six wholly-owned operating businesses. Between them they supply farm inputs and fuel, mill grain into flour, haul freight, clear and forward cargo through the borders, store and handle goods, and monitor stock pledged as collateral.

Each company is run by its own team, but on one shared platform — a single system for orders, inventory, finance, people and delivery tracking. That means fewer hand-offs between links in the chain, consolidated reporting for the group, and one point of accountability for a customer who needs more than one service.

What the group does

  • Input & fuel supply to farms and agri-business
  • Grain intake, milling and flour distribution
  • Road freight, distribution and last-mile delivery
  • Customs clearance and freight forwarding
  • Ambient & cold storage, cross-docking, handling
  • Collateral inspection and chain-of-custody monitoring
Our companies

Morise Agro Ltd

Agricultural inputs & fuel

Seed, fertiliser, agro-chemicals and fuel supplied to farms and agri-businesses across Uganda, from the Kira distribution base.

Morise Milling Ltd

Grain milling

Weighbridge grain intake and milling of maize, wheat, sorghum and millet, with packaging and distribution of flour and by-products from five regional mills.

Morise Logistics Ltd

Road freight & delivery

Scheduled road freight, distribution and last-mile delivery for group companies and third-party clients, based in Jinja.

 

Morise Clearing & Forwarding Ltd

Customs & freight forwarding

Customs clearance and air / ocean / road forwarding with bonded warehousing at Uganda’s main border posts and Entebbe.

Morise Warehouse Management Ltd

Storage & handling

Multi-branch ambient and cold storage, cross-docking and inventory handling across five regional warehouses.

Morise Collateral Management Ltd

Collateral management

Field inspection, physical stock verification and chain-of-custody monitoring of goods pledged as collateral, for banks and financiers.

One platform. Every company above runs on the same Morise system, so the group sees one consolidated picture — and a client deals with one Morise.
